
Analysis of spectrum on a random graph with a particular [[Géométrie]].
> Let $G$ be a **compact Lie group** \[...\] The random geometric graph on $G$ is the random graph $\Gamma_{geom}(N, L)$ whose vertices are $N$ random points $g_1, \dots , g_N$ chosen under the Haar measure of $G$ and whose edges are the pairs $\{gi, gj\}$ with $d(g_i, g_j) \leq L$, $d$ being the distance associated to the standard Riemannian structure on $G$.
